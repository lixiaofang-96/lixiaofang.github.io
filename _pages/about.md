Xiaofang Li received the B.S. degree from Wuhan University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, China, in 2018; and the Ph.D. degree in applied mathematics from Xidian University, Xi’an, China, in 2024. She is currently a Lecturer with the college of science, Northwest A&F University, Yangling, China. Her research interests include image processing and deep learning.
